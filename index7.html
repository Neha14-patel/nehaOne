Jamshedpur, also known as Tatanagar, is the largest industrial city in Jharkhand, India, famous as the "Steel
City of India".Purbi Singhbhum district is situated at the southeast corner of Jharkhand. It has been formed after isolating nine block from old Singhbhum on 16th January 1990. From the industrial growth and mining quarrying point of view, this district has a leading position in Jharkhand.
